Measurability codes:

- `measure_unspecified`: Requires monitoring, assessment, or documentation without naming a measure, cadence, or threshold.
- `capability_unmeasured`: Specifies a capability of the human overseer without a way to measure whether it is achieved.
- `trigger_unspecified`: Requires predefined triggers without a metric or threshold.
- `action_named`: Names an intervention type (for example recalibration) without a monitored signal that would trigger it.
- `principles_only`: Principle-level statement only.

## 2. Quoted text and notes

**R01** (S1, MEASURE 2.4 (Table 3)). "The functionality and behavior of the AI system and its components – as identified in the MAP function – are monitored when in production."
Note: Requires production monitoring of behavior. Names no measure, cadence, or threshold.

**R02** (S1, MEASURE 2.5 (Table 3)). "Limitations of the generalizability beyond the conditions under which the technology was developed are documented."
Note: Corresponds to documenting the validated operating envelope. Requires documentation, not monitoring against it.

**R03** (S1, MEASURE 3.1 (Table 3)). "to regularly identify and track existing, unanticipated, and emergent AI risks based on factors such as intended and actual performance in deployed contexts"
Note: Requires risk tracking over time from actual deployed performance. No indicators or detection delay named.

**R04** (S1, MANAGE 4.1 (Table 4)). "Post-deployment AI system monitoring plans are implemented, including mechanisms for capturing and evaluating input from users and other relevant AI actors, appeal and override, decommissioning, incident response, recovery, and change management."
Note: Names override, incident response, and change management as plan elements. Gives no effectiveness measure for any of them.

**R05** (S1, MAP 3.5 (Table 2)). "Processes for human oversight are defined, assessed, and documented in accordance with organizational policies from the GOVERN function."
Note: Oversight must be defined and assessed. The measure of assessment is left open, which is the gap EHOR addresses.

**R06** (S1, Measure function, discussion of measurement results). "Options may include recalibration, impact mitigation, or removal of the system from design, development, production, or use"
Note: Recalibration appears as a management option after measurement. It is an action, not a monitored calibration signal.

**R07** (S1, Operations tasks (AI lifecycle discussion)). "ongoing monitoring for periodic updates, testing, and subject matter expert (SME) recalibration of models, the tracking of incidents or errors reported and their management"
Note: Describes operations-phase tasks. Again names recalibration as a task and names no calibration measure.

**R08** (S1, AI RMF discussion of trustworthiness metrics). "Human judgment should be employed when deciding on the specific metrics related to AI trustworthiness characteristics and the precise threshold values for those metrics."
Note: Delegates metric and threshold choice to human judgment. Consistent with the instrument's rule that margins are application-specific and set in the VOE.

**R09** (S2, Art. 14(4)(a)). "properly understand the relevant capacities and limitations of the high-risk AI system"
Note: Specifies a capability of the overseer. States no way to measure whether it is achieved.

**R10** (S2, Art. 14(4)(b)). "to remain aware of the possible tendency of automatically relying or over-relying on the output produced by a high-risk AI system"
Note: Automation bias is named as a risk to manage. H4 (inappropriate acceptance) measures its consequence directly.

**R11** (S2, Art. 14(4)(c)). "correctly interpret the high-risk AI system"
Note: Correct interpretation of output is required of the overseer. No measure is specified.

**R12** (S2, Art. 72(1)). "shall establish and document a post-market monitoring system" / "in a manner that is proportionate to the nature of the AI technologies and the risks of the high-risk AI system"
Note: Requires a documented monitoring system, proportionate to risk. Proportionality supports risk-adaptive margins.

**R13** (S2, Art. 72(2)). "shall actively and systematically collect, document and analyse relevant data" / "throughout their lifetime"
Note: Requires active, systematic collection and analysis of performance data over the lifetime. Names no indicators.

**R14** (S3, Modification Protocol, four primary components). "data management practices, 2) re-training practices, 3) performance evaluation protocols, and 4) update procedures, including communication and transparency to users and real-world monitoring plans as applicable"
Note: The closest retrieved match to lifecycle assurance. It structures planned modifications, not continuous assurance of the deployed system.

**R15** (S3, Re-training practices). "identify any triggers for re-training (e.g., when the quantity of new data reaches a certain size or when a drift in data is observed over time)"
Note: Asks for predefined triggers and gives drift as an example. Defines no drift metric or threshold, and the only trigger action named is re-training.

**R16** (S3, Post-market surveillance). "The manufacturer should also describe their post-market surveillance plans and procedures, which may include real-world monitoring and notification requirements if the device does not function as intended pursuant to the authorized PCCP."
Note: Asks for surveillance plans and notification when the device does not function as intended. Leaves the detection method open.

**R17** (S4, Publication landing page (overview)). "six consensus principles to ensure AI works to the public benefit of all countries"
Note: Landing page only. The full guidance and its list of principles were not retrieved, so no principle is quoted or mapped.
