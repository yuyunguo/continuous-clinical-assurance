# One follow-up question for decision D2 (the false-alarm budget)

For each physician who answered the first sheet. About 2 minutes. Please answer on your own, without looking at anyone else's answer.

## Context

The monitoring system raises an alert when it thinks the AI tool's harm may have risen. Some alerts will be false alarms: the tool is fine, but the monitor fired anyway. Each alert, true or false, would go to your governance process (for example, a safety committee) for review. In the study, every monitoring approach is held to the same false-alarm rate, so that the comparison between them is fair. That rate is a choice, and it should reflect what your governance process can realistically absorb.
