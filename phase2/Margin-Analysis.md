Pooled harm-equivalent margin (mean over the medium and high cells of all pooled scenarios, SC09 included on its own subgroup baseline): delta = 0.5 gives 1.1 windows, delta = 1 gives 2.2 windows, delta = 2 gives 4.3 windows. A margin of 1 window therefore corresponds to about 0.46 baseline-windows of harm.

## 3. Resolution floor

Standard errors of the reduction in the pooled cells (150 replicates each): median 0.13 windows, largest 1.43. Smallest reduction detected with 80 percent power at a one-sided 5 percent level: median 0.32 windows, worst cell 3.55. A margin below the worst-cell figure cannot be told from zero in every cell at this replicate count.
