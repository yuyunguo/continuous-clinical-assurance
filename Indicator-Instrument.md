### C1. Population and case-mix drift

- **Matrix row:** Clinical Context (Context validity)
- **Governance objective:** Ensure AI remains within intended use
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Use outside validated population/indication
- **Definition:** Divergence between the current patient population (age, sex, condition mix, care setting, outcome prevalence) and the validation population defined in the VOE.
- **Formula:** `PSI = sum_k (p_k - q_k) * ln(p_k / q_k) per stratified covariate, where p is the current and q the validation proportion in bin k; prevalence shift = |pi_t - pi_0|`
- **Data required:** EHR demographics, encounter type, diagnosis codes, outcome prevalence
- **Frequency:** Weekly windows, rolling
- **Baseline:** Validation-cohort covariate and prevalence distribution recorded in the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: PSI or prevalence shift exceeds m_Y for any VOE-listed covariate. Orange: exceeds m_O. Red: exceeds m_R or any covariate falls outside its VOE range.
- **Detection method:** Windowed PSI/standardized mean difference with permutation-based confidence bounds; sequential change-point test on the drift statistic
- **Governance action:** yellow: Increase monitoring frequency; review affected subgroups; orange: Restrict use to in-envelope population; initiate revalidation; red: Suspend use for out-of-envelope population
- **Clinical importance:** A model applied to patients unlike its validation cohort has unknown reliability, and errors concentrate in the unrepresented group.
- **Validation:** Phase II perturbation with controlled case-mix shift; measure detection delay and false-alarm rate against injected shift time
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC01, SC02; negative control in SC04, SC05, SC07, SC08, SC09, SC10, SC11, SC12, SC13, SC14, SC15, SC16, SC17, SC18, SC20, SC21, SC22, SC23, SC24

### C2. Input distribution shift

- **Matrix row:** Robustness (Robustness)
- **Governance objective:** Maintain performance under expected variation
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Performance degradation under distribution shift
- **Definition:** Multivariate shift in model inputs (features, embeddings, or prompt content) relative to the validation inputs.
- **Formula:** `MMD^2(P_t, P_0) with a fixed kernel, or per-feature two-sample KS statistic with multiplicity correction`
- **Data required:** model input logs, validation input sample
- **Frequency:** Daily windows
- **Baseline:** Reference input sample stored with the validated model version
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: statistic exceeds m_Y with corrected p below the VOE alpha. Orange: exceeds m_O on any VOE-critical input. Red: exceeds m_R and coincides with a performance or calibration alert.
- **Detection method:** Two-sample tests with false-discovery-rate control; embedding-space drift for text inputs
- **Governance action:** yellow: Log and review contributing inputs; orange: Restrict use to inputs within reference support; investigate upstream change; red: Suspend affected pathway pending investigation
- **Clinical importance:** Input shift is often the earliest observable precursor of degraded output quality, before outcomes are available.
- **Validation:** Phase II injected covariate and temporal shifts; compare lead time against outcome-based performance monitoring
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC01, SC07, SC25; negative control in SC04, SC08, SC10, SC17, SC18, SC20, SC22, SC23, SC24

### C3. Intended-use conformance rate

- **Matrix row:** Clinical Context (Context validity)
- **Governance objective:** Ensure AI remains within intended use
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Use outside validated population/indication
- **Definition:** Proportion of AI invocations that occur within the indication, user role, care setting, and workflow step specified in the VOE.
- **Formula:** `conformance = N_in_scope / N_total invocations`
- **Data required:** invocation logs with user role, order/encounter context, workflow step
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Baseline:** Intended-use specification in the VOE; expected conformance set at go-live
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: conformance falls below 1 - m_Y. Orange: below 1 - m_O. Red: any use in a VOE-prohibited context.
- **Detection method:** Rule-based classification of each invocation with proportion control chart
- **Governance action:** yellow: Notify service owner; review access and training; orange: Restrict access by role and setting; red: Block prohibited invocation paths
- **Clinical importance:** Off-label use extends the model beyond any evidence of safety.
- **Validation:** Phase II simulated scope creep; measure detection of out-of-scope invocation share
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC19; negative control in SC07

### C4. Input missingness rate

- **Matrix row:** Data Quality (Input integrity)
- **Governance objective:** Ensure inputs remain fit for purpose
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Missing, corrupted, outdated, or biased inputs
- **Definition:** Proportion of required model inputs that are missing or defaulted at inference time, overall and per variable.
- **Formula:** `m_j = N_missing_j / N_invocations for each required variable j; overall = mean_j m_j`
- **Data required:** inference-time input records, required-variable list from VOE
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Baseline:** Missingness observed in validation, per variable
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: m_j exceeds baseline by m_Y for any VOE-critical variable. Orange: by m_O. Red: critical variable missing above m_R or systematically absent in a subgroup.
- **Detection method:** EWMA or CUSUM on per-variable missingness; subgroup stratification
- **Governance action:** yellow: Flag data owner; check upstream feed; orange: Route affected cases to human review; correct data pipeline; red: Suspend inference for cases missing critical inputs
- **Clinical importance:** Silent imputation can hide degraded input quality behind normal-looking outputs.
- **Validation:** Phase II injected missingness (random and informative); measure detection delay and downstream calibration effect
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC05, SC06; negative control in SC01, SC04, SC08, SC17, SC23, SC25

### C5. Data quality and freshness violations

- **Matrix row:** Data Quality (Input integrity)
- **Governance objective:** Ensure inputs remain fit for purpose
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Missing, corrupted, outdated, or biased inputs
- **Definition:** Rate of schema violations, out-of-range values, unit changes, duplicate records, and stale data (latency beyond the VOE limit) in model inputs.
- **Formula:** `violation rate = N_violations / N_records; staleness = fraction of records older than the VOE freshness limit`
- **Data required:** pipeline logs, schema definitions, measurement ranges, timestamps
- **Frequency:** Continuous, summarized daily
- **Baseline:** Violation and latency profile during validation
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: violation rate above m_Y. Orange: above m_O or any schema change. Red: unit or coding change affecting a critical variable.
- **Detection method:** Rule-based validation with control chart on violation rate; schema-diff monitoring
- **Governance action:** yellow: Notify data owner; orange: Quarantine affected records; correct pipeline; red: Suspend affected workflow until data integrity is restored
- **Clinical importance:** Corrupted or outdated inputs produce confident but wrong outputs.
- **Validation:** Phase II injected unit change, schema drift, and latency; measure detection delay by violation type
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC07; negative control in SC01, SC08, SC17, SC23, SC25

### P1. Sensitivity

- **Matrix row:** Performance (Predictive performance)
- **Governance objective:** Maintain clinical predictive reliability
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Increased false positives/negatives
- **Definition:** Proportion of true-positive cases correctly identified by the AI output at the deployed decision threshold.
- **Formula:** `Se = TP / (TP + FN) in each monitoring window`
- **Data required:** predictions, adjudicated outcomes
- **Frequency:** Monthly, or per fixed case count when outcomes lag
- **Baseline:** Validated sensitivity with confidence interval in the VOE

- **Detection method:** Sequential probability ratio test or CUSUM on the binomial proportion; outcome-lag adjustment

- **Clinical importance:** Missed true cases translate directly into delayed diagnosis or treatment.
- **Validation:** Phase II label-shift and concept-drift injection; detection delay versus injected onset

### P3. Positive and negative predictive value

- **Matrix row:** Performance (Predictive performance)
- **Governance objective:** Maintain clinical predictive reliability
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Increased false positives/negatives
- **Definition:** Probability that a positive (negative) output is correct in the current population, which varies with prevalence.
- **Formula:** `PPV = TP / (TP + FP); NPV = TN / (TN + FN)`
- **Data required:** predictions, adjudicated outcomes, prevalence
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline:** Validated PPV and NPV at the VOE prevalence
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: PPV or NPV falls below baseline by m_Y after prevalence adjustment. Orange: m_O. Red: below VOE minimum.
- **Detection method:** Sequential test with prevalence-standardized comparison; report raw and standardized values
- **Governance action:** yellow: Interpret against prevalence shift (link to C1); orange: Revalidate for the current prevalence; red: Restrict use
- **Clinical importance:** Predictive values determine what a clinician can conclude from an output at the bedside.
- **Validation:** Phase II prevalence shift with fixed Se/Sp; confirm that PPV/NPV change is attributed to C1 and not to model degradation
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC02; negative control in none

### P4. Discrimination (AUROC)

- **Matrix row:** Performance (Predictive performance)
- **Governance objective:** Maintain clinical predictive reliability
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Increased false positives/negatives
- **Definition:** Ability of model scores to rank cases with the outcome above cases without it.
- **Formula:** `AUROC = P(s_i > s_j | y_i = 1, y_j = 0); bootstrap CI per window`
- **Data required:** scores, adjudicated outcomes
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline:** Validated AUROC and CI in the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: lower CI bound below baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: below VOE minimum discrimination.
- **Detection method:** Bootstrap CI monitoring; DeLong-type comparison to baseline; CUSUM on windowed estimates
- **Governance action:** yellow: Increase monitoring; check inputs (C2); orange: Revalidate or retrain; red: Suspend use
- **Clinical importance:** Loss of ranking ability means the model no longer separates high-risk from low-risk patients. It serves as the comparator for calibration-based early warning (RH2).
- **Validation:** Phase II tests RH2 directly, comparing calibration drift with stable AUROC against AUROC drift, and detection delay across the two
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC04, SC08, SC17, SC25; negative control in SC10, SC11, SC12, SC13, SC14, SC15, SC16

### P5. Severity-weighted error rate

- **Matrix row:** Patient Safety (Safety event rate)
- **Governance objective:** Detect clinically consequential failures
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Adverse event/near miss
- **Definition:** Error rate weighted by adjudicated potential or actual clinical severity, so that a rare severe error outweighs many minor ones.
- **Formula:** `SWER = sum_e w(sev_e) / N_cases, with weights w defined in the VOE`
- **Data required:** adjudicated errors with severity grade, case counts
- **Frequency:** Monthly, with immediate reporting of top-severity errors
- **Baseline:** SWER during validation and the VOE severity weights
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: SWER above baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: any error at the highest severity grade or SWER above m_R.
- **Detection method:** Control chart on weighted rate; count-based sentinel rule for top-severity errors
- **Governance action:** yellow: Review error cases with clinical safety officer; orange: Restrict use; root-cause analysis; red: Suspend pending investigation
- **Clinical importance:** Aggregate accuracy hides the errors that harm patients most.
- **Validation:** Phase II severity-labeled synthetic errors; compare detection of severe-error rise against aggregate error rate
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC08; negative control in none

### P6. Subgroup performance gap

- **Matrix row:** Equity (Subgroup reliability)
- **Governance objective:** Maintain acceptable subgroup performance
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Differential performance
- **Definition:** Difference in performance (sensitivity, calibration, or error rate) between predefined subgroups, such as sex, age band, ethnicity, and site.
- **Formula:** `gap_g = M_g - M_ref for metric M and subgroup g; report max |gap| across subgroups with CI`
- **Data required:** predictions, outcomes, subgroup attributes
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline:** Validated subgroup gaps in the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: any gap exceeds baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: gap exceeds the VOE maximum acceptable disparity.
- **Detection method:** Stratified monitoring with multiplicity control; interaction test for gap change over time
- **Governance action:** yellow: Increase monitoring for the affected subgroup; orange: Revalidate within subgroup; mitigation plan; red: Restrict use in the affected subgroup
- **Clinical importance:** Overall performance can remain stable while one group is harmed.
- **Validation:** Phase II subgroup-specific drift; measure detection delay for a subgroup versus the pooled metric
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC06, SC09; negative control in none

### U1. Expected calibration error

- **Matrix row:** Calibration (Calibration)
- **Governance objective:** Ensure confidence reflects actual reliability
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Overconfidence or underconfidence
- **Definition:** Average gap between stated confidence and observed correctness across confidence bins.
- **Formula:** `ECE = sum_b (n_b / N) * |acc_b - conf_b| over B bins; report the binning scheme and a bootstrap CI`
- **Data required:** confidence scores, adjudicated outcomes
- **Frequency:** Monthly, or per fixed case count
- **Baseline:** Validated ECE with CI in the VOE (recalibrated model if a recalibration layer is deployed)
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: lower CI bound of ECE exceeds baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: exceeds m_R with a concurrent U4 or P5 alert.
- **Detection method:** Windowed ECE with bootstrap CI; CUSUM on the ECE series; binning sensitivity check
- **Governance action:** yellow: Increase human review of high-confidence outputs; orange: Recalibrate; revalidate; red: Restrict or suspend the affected use case
- **Clinical importance:** Clinicians calibrate trust to stated confidence, so overconfidence converts model error into accepted error.
- **Validation:** Phase II calibration-drift injection at stable AUROC (tests RH2); compare detection delay with P4 and P1/P2
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC02, SC08, SC17, SC23, SC25; negative control in SC10, SC11, SC12, SC13, SC14, SC15, SC16, SC18, SC21, SC22, SC24

### U2. Brier score

- **Matrix row:** Calibration (Calibration)
- **Governance objective:** Ensure confidence reflects actual reliability
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Overconfidence or underconfidence
- **Definition:** Mean squared difference between predicted probability and observed outcome, combining calibration and refinement.
- **Formula:** `BS = (1/N) * sum_i (p_i - y_i)^2; decompose into reliability, resolution, and uncertainty`
- **Data required:** predicted probabilities, outcomes
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline:** Validated Brier score and decomposition in the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: BS above baseline by m_Y with reliability term driving the change. Orange: by m_O. Red: above VOE maximum.
- **Detection method:** Windowed BS with bootstrap CI; monitor the reliability component separately
- **Governance action:** yellow: Investigate reliability versus resolution change; orange: Recalibrate or revalidate; red: Restrict use
- **Clinical importance:** A proper scoring rule that penalizes confident errors, so it complements ECE without depending on binning.
- **Validation:** Phase II comparison of detection delay and false-alarm rate against ECE
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC23; negative control in none

### U3. Calibration slope and intercept

- **Matrix row:** Calibration (Calibration)
- **Governance objective:** Ensure confidence reflects actual reliability
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Overconfidence or underconfidence
- **Definition:** Parameters of a logistic recalibration of outcomes on the model's logit score, showing systematic over- or underestimation and over- or under-dispersion.
- **Formula:** `logit(P(y=1)) = a + b * logit(p); ideal a = 0, b = 1`
- **Data required:** predicted probabilities, outcomes
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline:** Validated intercept and slope with CI in the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: CI of slope or intercept excludes the VOE band by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: slope below m_R (severe overconfidence) or intercept beyond the VOE bound.
- **Detection method:** Logistic recalibration per window with CI; sequential monitoring of both parameters
- **Governance action:** yellow: Flag for recalibration review; orange: Apply recalibration update under change control; red: Restrict use until revalidated
- **Clinical importance:** Slope below 1 signals overconfident risk estimates, a direct safety concern for threshold-based decisions.
- **Validation:** Phase II injected slope and intercept drift; verify that recalibration restores the VOE band
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC02, SC08, SC23, SC25; negative control in SC10, SC18, SC24

### U4. High-confidence error rate

- **Matrix row:** Uncertainty (Uncertainty reliability)
- **Governance objective:** Ensure uncertain cases are appropriately managed
- **Clinical or operational risk:** AI produces high-confidence errors
- **Definition:** Proportion of errors that occur at confidence above a VOE-defined high-confidence cutoff.
- **Formula:** `HCER = N(error and conf >= tau) / N(conf >= tau)`
- **Data required:** confidence scores, adjudicated outcomes
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline:** Validated HCER in the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: HCER above baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: above VOE maximum or any high-severity error in the high-confidence region.
- **Detection method:** Binomial control chart within the high-confidence stratum
- **Governance action:** yellow: Route high-confidence outputs for spot review; orange: Mandatory verification of high-confidence outputs; red: Suspend automated acceptance
- **Clinical importance:** Errors made with high confidence are the least likely to be challenged by clinicians.
- **Validation:** Phase II overconfidence injection; measure detection delay and link to H4 inappropriate acceptance
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC20, SC23; negative control in none

### U5. Confidence distribution shift

- **Matrix row:** Uncertainty (Uncertainty reliability)
- **Governance objective:** Ensure uncertain cases are appropriately managed
- **Clinical or operational risk:** AI produces high-confidence errors
- **Definition:** Change in the distribution of model confidence, which can shift before outcomes are available.
- **Formula:** `Wasserstein or KS distance between current and reference confidence distributions; mean and dispersion change`
- **Data required:** confidence scores
- **Frequency:** Daily windows
- **Baseline:** Reference confidence distribution from validation
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: distance above m_Y. Orange: above m_O. Red: above m_R together with a C2 alert.
- **Detection method:** Two-sample distance test with FDR control; requires no outcome labels, so it is usable when outcomes lag
- **Governance action:** yellow: Increase calibration monitoring and sampling for adjudication; orange: Prioritize outcome adjudication; investigate inputs; red: Restrict use pending calibration check
- **Clinical importance:** It is a label-free early signal that calibration may be deteriorating.
- **Validation:** Phase II lead time of U5 alerts over U1 alerts under injected drift, and false-alarm rate
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC20, SC23, SC25; negative control in none

### U6. Abstention and selective risk

- **Matrix row:** Uncertainty (Uncertainty reliability)
- **Governance objective:** Ensure uncertain cases are appropriately managed
- **Clinical or operational risk:** AI produces high-confidence errors
- **Definition:** Rate at which the system defers or abstains, and the error rate on the cases it answers (selective risk).
- **Formula:** `abstention = N_abstain / N_total; selective risk = errors among answered / N_answered`
- **Data required:** outputs with abstain flag, outcomes
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Baseline:** Validated coverage and selective-risk curve in the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: abstention or selective risk moves from baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: selective risk above VOE maximum at current coverage.
- **Detection method:** Control charts on both quantities; risk-coverage curve comparison
- **Governance action:** yellow: Review abstention rule and workload; orange: Adjust deferral threshold under change control; red: Suspend automated answers; route all to human review
- **Clinical importance:** Abstention that stops working removes the safety net for uncertain cases.
- **Validation:** Phase II uncertainty-estimator degradation; measure change in selective risk and detection delay
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC20; negative control in none

### G1. Groundedness

- **Matrix row:** LLM/RAG (Groundedness)
- **Governance objective:** Maintain grounded generation
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Hallucination/unsupported recommendation
- **Definition:** Proportion of generated clinical statements that are supported by the retrieved or provided source record.
- **Formula:** `groundedness = N_supported_claims / N_claims, judged by sampled clinician or validated automated audit`
- **Data required:** outputs, retrieved sources, audit labels
- **Frequency:** Weekly sampled audit
- **Baseline:** Validated groundedness with CI in the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: lower CI bound below baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: below VOE minimum or any unsupported high-risk claim.
- **Detection method:** Stratified sampling audit with control chart on the supported proportion; audit-method agreement check
- **Governance action:** yellow: Increase audit sampling; orange: Require human verification of claims; correct retrieval; red: Suspend generation for the affected task
- **Clinical importance:** Unsupported statements in a clinical note or recommendation can propagate into decisions.
- **Validation:** Follow-on generative or agentic study (outside Phase II) of injected retrieval degradation and prompt change; detection delay and audit-agreement analysis, including a check for evaluator dependence when the audit is automated
- **Phase II scenarios:** none (outside the Phase II predictive anchor)

### G2. Unsupported-claim rate

- **Matrix row:** LLM/RAG (Groundedness)
- **Governance objective:** Maintain grounded generation
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Hallucination/unsupported recommendation
- **Definition:** Rate of generated claims that are contradicted by, or absent from, the source record and are clinically material, weighted by severity.
- **Formula:** `UCR = N_unsupported_material_claims / N_outputs, with severity-weighted variant`
- **Data required:** outputs, source records, severity grades
- **Frequency:** Weekly sampled audit
- **Baseline:** Validated UCR in the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: UCR above baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: any unsupported claim at the highest severity.
- **Detection method:** Count and rate control chart; sentinel rule for top-severity claims
- **Governance action:** yellow: Increase audit sampling; orange: Human verification of all outputs in the affected task; red: Suspend generation pending investigation
- **Clinical importance:** Hallucinated clinical content is a distinct failure mode with no analogue in accuracy metrics.
- **Validation:** Follow-on generative or agentic study (outside Phase II) of seeded unsupported-claim injection; measure sensitivity of the audit sampling plan, including a check for evaluator dependence when the audit is automated
- **Phase II scenarios:** none (outside the Phase II predictive anchor)

### G3. Retrieval relevance

- **Matrix row:** LLM/RAG (Groundedness)
- **Governance objective:** Maintain grounded generation
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Hallucination/unsupported recommendation
- **Definition:** Relevance and sufficiency of retrieved passages for the query, at the ranks the generator uses.
- **Formula:** `precision@k and recall@k against adjudicated relevant sources for a sampled query set`
- **Data required:** queries, retrieved passages, relevance judgments
- **Frequency:** Weekly on a fixed probe set plus sampled live queries
- **Baseline:** Probe-set retrieval metrics at validation
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: precision@k or recall@k below baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: below VOE minimum on a safety-critical query class.
- **Detection method:** Fixed probe-set regression test; control chart on sampled live queries
- **Governance action:** yellow: Review corpus and index changes; orange: Correct retrieval or corpus; require human verification; red: Suspend retrieval-dependent generation
- **Clinical importance:** Degraded retrieval is a leading cause of ungrounded output and precedes G1 and G2 deterioration.
- **Validation:** Follow-on generative or agentic study (outside Phase II) of corpus and index perturbation; lead time of G3 over G1/G2 alerts
- **Phase II scenarios:** none (outside the Phase II predictive anchor)

### H1. Review coverage

- **Matrix row:** Human Oversight (Effective human oversight)
- **Governance objective:** Ensure meaningful human control
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Human misses AI error
- **Definition:** Proportion of AI outputs that require human review under the VOE and are actually reviewed before use.
- **Formula:** `coverage = N_reviewed_required / N_required_review`
- **Data required:** AI outputs, review-required flag, review event logs
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Baseline:** Required review policy in the VOE; coverage at go-live
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: coverage below 1 - m_Y. Orange: below 1 - m_O. Red: unreviewed outputs in a category the VOE marks as mandatory review.
- **Detection method:** Proportion control chart; cross-check of review logs against output logs
- **Governance action:** yellow: Notify supervisors; check reviewer workload; orange: Enforce hard-stop review for the affected category; red: Block use of unreviewed outputs
- **Clinical importance:** Documented human oversight means nothing when review is bypassed. Coverage is also the exposure denominator for interpreting H5.
- **Validation:** Phase II simulated review bypass; detection delay and effect on H5
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC11; negative control in SC01, SC02, SC05, SC06, SC07, SC09, SC25

### H2. Override rate

- **Matrix row:** Human Oversight (Appropriate acceptance)
- **Governance objective:** Prevent inappropriate acceptance
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Automation bias
- **Definition:** Proportion of reviewed AI outputs that the human changes or rejects.
- **Formula:** `override rate = N_overridden / N_reviewed`
- **Data required:** review decisions, final decisions
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Baseline:** Override rate observed during validation or pilot
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: rate departs from baseline by m_Y in either direction. Orange: by m_O. Red: sustained rate near zero when the error rate is nonzero, or an abrupt rise above m_R.
- **Detection method:** Two-sided control chart; interpret jointly with H3 and H4
- **Governance action:** yellow: Review the pattern by reviewer and case type; orange: Investigate rubber-stamping or distrust; retrain reviewers; red: Enforce independent verification
- **Clinical importance:** A falling override rate can indicate automation bias, a rising one loss of trust or model degradation. Neither direction is interpretable without H3.
- **Validation:** Phase II simulated reviewer-behavior models (bias, fatigue); joint interpretation with H3/H4
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC10, SC12; negative control in SC14

### H3. Appropriate override rate

- **Matrix row:** Human Oversight (Appropriate acceptance)
- **Governance objective:** Prevent inappropriate acceptance
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Automation bias
- **Definition:** Proportion of overrides that corrected an AI error, among all overrides.
- **Formula:** `AOR = N_overrides_correcting_error / N_overrides`
- **Data required:** override events, adjudicated ground truth
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline:** AOR observed during validation or pilot
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: AOR below baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: AOR below m_R, meaning most overrides introduce error.
- **Detection method:** Binomial control chart with adjudication-coverage reporting
- **Governance action:** yellow: Review inappropriate overrides; orange: Reviewer training; decision-support redesign; red: Restrict reviewer discretion pending review
- **Clinical importance:** Overrides that replace a correct AI output with a wrong human one are a harm the AI did not cause.
- **Validation:** Phase II simulated reviewer accuracy; agreement with adjudicated ground truth
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC12; negative control in none

### H4. Inappropriate acceptance rate

- **Matrix row:** Human Oversight (Appropriate acceptance)
- **Governance objective:** Prevent inappropriate acceptance
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Automation bias
- **Definition:** Proportion of AI errors that the human accepted, among reviewed AI errors (an automation-bias measure).
- **Formula:** `IAR = N_errors_accepted / N_errors_reviewed`
- **Data required:** AI outputs, adjudicated errors, final decisions
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline:** IAR observed during validation or pilot
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: IAR above baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: IAR above m_R, or acceptance of any highest-severity error.
- **Detection method:** Binomial control chart; stratify by confidence level and severity
- **Governance action:** yellow: Increase spot checks of accepted high-confidence outputs; orange: Mandatory independent verification for the affected case type; red: Suspend automated acceptance pathways
- **Clinical importance:** It measures directly how often AI errors reach patients through unchallenged acceptance.
- **Validation:** Phase II simulated overconfidence and automation bias; link to U4
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC10; negative control in none

### H5. Effective Human Oversight Rate (EHOR)

- **Matrix row:** Human Oversight (Effective human oversight)
- **Governance objective:** Ensure meaningful human control
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Human misses AI error
- **Definition:** Proportion of AI errors requiring human detection (adjudicated severity at or above the VOE cutoff s*, judged blind to reviewer action) that the reviewer intercepted before the workflow's point of no return. Errors that bypassed review or were reviewed too late stay in the denominator. It is estimated from an independent probability-sample audit and is always reported with the inappropriate-intervention rate (IIR), so that indiscriminate rejection cannot inflate it.
- **Formula:** `EHOR = sum_i w_i*I_i / sum_i w_i*R_i over audited outputs, with w_i = 1/pi_i (audit inclusion probability), R_i = 1 for an error of severity >= s*, and I_i = 1 only if R_i = 1 and the reviewer acted before the point of no return. IIR = sum_i w_i*Z_i / sum_i w_i*V_i, where V_i = 1 for a correct output that was reviewed and Z_i = 1 if it was wrongly rejected or changed. Decomposition: EHOR = Cov_R * EHOR_c, with Cov_R = P(reviewed in time | R=1) and EHOR_c = P(intercepted | R=1, reviewed in time). EHOR_c is the conditional variant and must be labeled as such.`
- **Data required:** AI outputs, independent adjudication of a probability sample of outputs blind to reviewer action, audit inclusion probabilities, review logs with timestamps, final decisions, point-of-no-return definition per workflow, count of sampled outputs not adjudicated
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline:** EHOR and IIR from a validation reader study with seeded or adjudicated errors, stratified by severity; audit design and s* fixed in the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: lower CI bound of EHOR below baseline by m_Y at unchanged IIR. Orange: by m_O. Red: EHOR below m_R in the highest-severity stratum. A rise in EHOR accompanied by an IIR rise beyond m_Y is flagged as indiscriminate rejection, not improvement.
- **Detection method:** Design-weighted proportion with bootstrap CI by severity stratum; sequential monitoring of the weighted estimate; bounds that treat sampled but unadjudicated outputs as all intercepted (upper) or all missed (lower), reported with the unadjudicated fraction
- **Governance action:** yellow: Additional supervision or training; orange: Workflow intervention; second reviewer for the affected stratum; red: Restrict or suspend the affected AI function
- **Clinical importance:** It answers whether the human in the loop actually catches the errors that matter (RH3).
- **Validation:** Phase II/III reader simulation with seeded errors; test whether EHOR adds information beyond documented review presence and H1; compare the design-weighted estimator with a naive estimator that counts only errors found through review, to quantify ascertainment bias
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC10, SC11; negative control in none

### H6. Intervention latency

- **Matrix row:** Human Oversight (Intervention latency)
- **Governance objective:** Preserve ability to intervene
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Reviewer cannot act in time
- **Definition:** Time from AI output (or alert) to human review and, where needed, corrective intervention.
- **Formula:** `latency = t_review - t_output; report median and 95th percentile against the clinical time window`
- **Data required:** output timestamps, review and action timestamps
- **Frequency:** Continuous, summarized weekly
- **Baseline:** Validated latency distribution and clinical time windows in the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: 95th percentile exceeds baseline by m_Y. Orange: exceeds the clinical window in more than m_O of cases. Red: time-critical output unreviewed beyond its window.
- **Detection method:** Quantile control chart; survival analysis of time to review
- **Governance action:** yellow: Review staffing and routing; orange: Escalation and reallocation of reviewers; red: Automatic escalation and interim safe default
- **Clinical importance:** An intervention that arrives after the clinical window cannot prevent harm.
- **Validation:** Phase II queue and staffing perturbations; measure effect on H5
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC13; negative control in none

### H7. Escalation compliance

- **Matrix row:** Workflow Safety (Workflow integrity)
- **Governance objective:** Preserve safe workflow execution
- **Clinical or operational risk:** AI disrupts or bypasses clinical process
- **Definition:** Proportion of cases meeting VOE escalation criteria (high risk, low confidence, disagreement) that were escalated to the designated senior reviewer.
- **Formula:** `compliance = N_escalated_required / N_escalation_required`
- **Data required:** escalation criteria flags, escalation logs
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Baseline:** Escalation policy in the VOE; compliance at go-live
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: compliance below 1 - m_Y. Orange: below 1 - m_O. Red: missed escalation of a highest-severity case.
- **Detection method:** Proportion control chart with sentinel rule on top-severity misses
- **Governance action:** yellow: Notify service owner; orange: Automate escalation routing; audit; red: Suspend affected function until escalation path is restored
- **Clinical importance:** Escalation is how uncertain or high-risk cases reach the person with authority to act.
- **Validation:** Phase III intervention experiment; effect of automated escalation on missed-risk events
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC14; negative control in none

### O1. Workflow completion rate

- **Matrix row:** Workflow Safety (Workflow integrity)
- **Governance objective:** Preserve safe workflow execution
- **Clinical or operational risk:** AI disrupts or bypasses clinical process
- **Definition:** Proportion of AI-initiated workflow episodes that reach their intended endpoint, with task abandonment reported as the complement.
- **Formula:** `completion = N_completed / N_initiated; abandonment = 1 - completion`
- **Data required:** workflow system event logs
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Baseline:** Completion rate at go-live or pilot
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: completion falls below baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: completion failure in a safety-critical workflow step.

- **Governance action:** yellow: Review workflow with operations; orange: Workflow redesign or interim manual fallback; red: Suspend AI step in the affected workflow

### O2. Escalation failure rate

- **Matrix row:** Workflow Safety (Workflow integrity)
- **Governance objective:** Preserve safe workflow execution
- **Clinical or operational risk:** AI disrupts or bypasses clinical process
- **Definition:** Proportion of AI-generated escalations that fail to reach the responsible clinician, or reach them after the clinical window.
- **Formula:** `failure rate = N_failed_or_late_escalations / N_escalations`
- **Data required:** escalation events, delivery and acknowledgment timestamps
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Baseline:** Escalation reliability at go-live
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: failure rate above baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: any failed escalation for a highest-severity case.
- **Detection method:** Proportion control chart with sentinel rule; end-to-end synthetic test escalations
- **Governance action:** yellow: Inspect routing and contact directory; orange: Repair routing; add redundant channel; red: Suspend AI-dependent escalation; revert to manual

### O3. Alert burden

- **Matrix row:** Alert Burden (Alert burden)
- **Governance objective:** Prevent excessive cognitive load
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Alert fatigue
- **Definition:** Alert volume per user per unit time, and the proportion of alerts acted on, as indicators of alert fatigue.
- **Formula:** `burden = alerts / (user * shift); action rate = N_actioned / N_alerts`
- **Data required:** alert logs, user actions
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Baseline:** Burden and action rate at go-live
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: burden above baseline by m_Y or action rate below baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: burden above the VOE maximum tolerable level.
- **Detection method:** Longitudinal control charts; per-user distribution monitoring
- **Governance action:** yellow: Review alert thresholds; orange: Threshold redesign; suppress low-value alerts; red: Suspend nonessential alerting
- **Clinical importance:** Alert fatigue leads clinicians to ignore correct alerts.
- **Validation:** Phase II alert-volume perturbation; link to H2 and H4
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC15; negative control in none

### O4. Turnaround time

- **Matrix row:** Workflow Safety (Workflow integrity)
- **Governance objective:** Preserve safe workflow execution
- **Clinical or operational risk:** AI disrupts or bypasses clinical process
- **Definition:** Time for the AI-supported process to deliver its result to the point of care, compared with the service-level requirement.
- **Formula:** `TAT = t_delivered - t_request; median and 95th percentile against the VOE service limit`
- **Data required:** request and delivery timestamps
- **Frequency:** Continuous, summarized daily
- **Baseline:** Validated TAT distribution and service limit in the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: 95th percentile above baseline by m_Y. Orange: service limit exceeded in more than m_O of cases. Red: delay in a time-critical pathway.
- **Detection method:** Quantile control chart
- **Governance action:** yellow: Investigate system load and integration; orange: Scale or reroute; interim manual pathway; red: Suspend AI step and revert to manual pathway
- **Clinical importance:** Delay in a time-critical pathway can outweigh the benefit of a correct output.
- **Validation:** Phase II latency perturbation; detection delay
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC16; negative control in none

### O5. Downstream error rate

- **Matrix row:** Workflow Safety (Workflow integrity)
- **Governance objective:** Preserve safe workflow execution
- **Clinical or operational risk:** AI disrupts or bypasses clinical process
- **Definition:** Rate of errors in later workflow steps (orders, documentation, communication) traceable to an AI output.
- **Formula:** `downstream error rate = N_errors_traced_to_AI_output / N_AI_influenced_episodes`
- **Data required:** workflow logs, incident and correction records, AI output links
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline:** Rate in validation or pilot
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: rate above baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: any downstream error at the highest severity.
- **Detection method:** Control chart with trace-based attribution audit
- **Governance action:** yellow: Sample and review traced episodes; orange: Workflow redesign; add verification step; red: Suspend AI influence on the affected step
- **Clinical importance:** Harm frequently arises after the AI step, where model-level metrics do not reach.
- **Validation:** Phase II injected downstream propagation; test attribution accuracy
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC22; negative control in none

### O6. Near-miss and sentinel event rate

- **Matrix row:** Patient Safety (Safety event rate)
- **Governance objective:** Detect clinically consequential failures
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Adverse event/near miss
- **Definition:** Rate of reported near misses and sentinel events in which an AI output contributed, classified by harm severity.
- **Formula:** `event rate = N_events_AI_contributed / N_AI_influenced_episodes, by severity class`
- **Data required:** safety reporting system, EHR, incident classification
- **Frequency:** Continuous reporting; monthly rate
- **Baseline:** Pre-deployment event rate for the process and the validation-period rate
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: near-miss rate above baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: any sentinel event with AI contribution.
- **Detection method:** Sentinel rule for sentinel events; count-based control chart for near misses; under-reporting audit
- **Governance action:** yellow: Safety review of near misses; orange: Restrict use; formal investigation; red: Suspend and initiate serious-event review
- **Clinical importance:** It is the closest direct measure of clinical harm, but it is sparse and under-reported, so it cannot serve as the only detector.
- **Validation:** Phase III retrospective linkage between earlier alerts and later events; time-to-detection versus outcome-based detection
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC22; negative control in none

### G4. Action policy-violation rate

- **Matrix row:** Agentic AI (Action compliance)
- **Governance objective:** Constrain autonomous action
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Unauthorized or unsafe action
- **Definition:** Rate at which agent actions (orders, scheduling, messages to patients) violate clinical policy or safety rules, whether or not within granted permissions.
- **Formula:** `violation rate = N_actions_violating_policy / N_actions, by action class and severity`
- **Data required:** agent action logs, policy rule set, adjudicated review
- **Frequency:** Continuous with weekly audit
- **Baseline:** Validated violation rate in simulated and pilot use
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: rate above baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: any violation with patient-facing or irreversible effect.
- **Detection method:** Rule-based policy check with audit sampling; sentinel rule for high-severity actions
- **Governance action:** yellow: Review agent behavior for the action class; orange: Require human approval for the action class; red: Halt the agent and escalate
- **Clinical importance:** An agent that acts can cause harm without any human seeing the output first.
- **Validation:** Follow-on generative or agentic study (outside Phase II) of agent simulation with seeded unsafe actions; sensitivity of rule checks and audit sampling
- **Phase II scenarios:** none (outside the Phase II predictive anchor)

### G5. Trajectory failure and recovery

- **Matrix row:** Agentic AI (Trajectory integrity)
- **Governance objective:** Maintain safe reasoning trajectory
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Multi-step reasoning failure
- **Definition:** Rate of failed reasoning or tool-use steps within an agent trajectory (input, retrieval, planning, tool use, action, observation) and the rate at which the agent recovers or escalates appropriately.
- **Formula:** `failed step rate = N_failed_steps / N_steps; recovery rate = N_recovered_or_escalated / N_failed_steps; trace tau = (I, R, P, X, A, O) for input, retrieval, planning, tool execution, action, observation`
- **Data required:** step-level agent trajectory logs, tool error codes
- **Frequency:** Continuous, summarized weekly
- **Baseline:** Step-level failure and recovery profile in validation
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: failed step rate above baseline by m_Y or recovery rate below baseline by m_Y. Orange: by m_O. Red: unrecovered failure at a critical step (action or patient communication).
- **Detection method:** Step-level monitoring with control charts; trajectory reconstruction on sampled episodes
- **Governance action:** yellow: Review failing step type; orange: Reduce autonomy; add checkpoint approval; red: Halt trajectory and escalate to a human
- **Clinical importance:** Multi-step failures compound, and an early step error can invalidate a later correct-looking action.
- **Validation:** Follow-on generative or agentic study (outside Phase II) of perturbation of tool reliability and retrieval; detection of compounding failure
- **Phase II scenarios:** none (outside the Phase II predictive anchor)

### L1. Version-change impact

- **Matrix row:** Lifecycle (Change impact)
- **Governance objective:** Control changes to AI system
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Update introduces unexpected behavior
- **Definition:** Change in performance, calibration, and error profile attributable to a model version update, measured against the prior version on the same cases.
- **Formula:** `delta_M = M(v_new) - M(v_old) on a shared case set, for M in {Se, Sp, AUROC, ECE, SWER}, with paired CI`
- **Data required:** version registry, paired outputs, outcomes or reference labels
- **Frequency:** At each release and for a defined post-release window
- **Baseline:** Prior validated version within the VOE
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: any delta_M beyond m_Y. Orange: beyond m_O. Red: beyond m_R or regression in a VOE-critical subgroup.
- **Detection method:** Paired comparison and shadow-mode evaluation; post-release sequential monitoring
- **Governance action:** yellow: Extend shadow mode; staged rollout; orange: Hold release; revalidate; red: Roll back to the prior validated version
- **Clinical importance:** Updates are a common route by which validated safety is silently lost.
- **Validation:** Phase II simulated updates with known regressions; detection delay and rollback effectiveness in Phase III
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC17; negative control in SC01, SC02, SC05, SC06, SC07, SC08, SC09, SC19, SC20, SC23, SC25

### L2. Prompt, configuration, and corpus change events

- **Matrix row:** Lifecycle (Change impact)
- **Governance objective:** Control changes to AI system
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Update introduces unexpected behavior
- **Definition:** Count and classification of changes to prompts, system configuration, tools, retrieval corpora, and model settings, and their linkage to post-change monitoring results.
- **Formula:** `change events per period by class; fraction followed by a completed regression check within the VOE window`
- **Data required:** configuration and prompt versioning system, corpus index logs, tool registry
- **Frequency:** Continuous
- **Baseline:** VOE inventory of approved system components and versions
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: any change without a linked regression check within m_Y days. Orange: unapproved change to a VOE-controlled component. Red: change to a safety-critical component deployed without approval.
- **Detection method:** Automated diff of configuration against the approved VOE inventory; audit of change tickets
- **Governance action:** yellow: Complete regression check; orange: Revert unapproved change; retrospective review; red: Roll back and suspend until change-control review
- **Clinical importance:** For generative and agentic systems, behavior changes through prompts, tools, and knowledge sources even when model weights are fixed.
- **Validation:** Follow-on generative or agentic study (outside Phase II) of prompt and corpus perturbations; detection of unapproved changes and downstream effect on G1-G3
- **Phase II scenarios:** none (outside the Phase II predictive anchor)

### L3. Trace completeness

- **Matrix row:** Traceability (Decision traceability)
- **Governance objective:** Enable retrospective audit
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Cannot reconstruct AI decision
- **Definition:** Proportion of AI decisions for which the full trace (input, version, prompt, retrieval, tool use, output, reviewer action) can be reconstructed.
- **Formula:** `completeness = N_fully_traceable / N_decisions; per-element completeness reported`
- **Data required:** system logs, audit sample
- **Frequency:** Weekly audit sample
- **Baseline:** VOE minimum required trace elements
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: completeness below 1 - m_Y. Orange: below 1 - m_O. Red: missing trace for any safety event.
- **Detection method:** Audit sampling with proportion control chart; reconstruction test on sampled decisions
- **Governance action:** yellow: Logging remediation ticket; orange: Fix logging before further release; red: Suspend affected function until traceability is restored
- **Clinical importance:** Without a trace, an adverse event cannot be investigated and no other indicator can be audited.
- **Validation:** Phase II log-degradation injection; reconstruction success rate
- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC21; negative control in none

### L4. Change-control compliance

- **Clinical or operational risk:** Update introduces unexpected behavior

### L5. Accountability completeness

- **Matrix row:** Governance (Accountability completeness)
- **Governance objective:** Maintain accountability
- **Clinical or operational risk:** Unclear responsibility
- **Definition:** Proportion of the accountability elements the VOE requires (named owner, escalation path, decision authority, and a reachable on-call contact) that are assigned, current, and reachable for the deployed system.
- **Formula:** `completeness = N_elements_current_and_reachable / N_elements_required`
- **Data required:** governance records
- **Frequency:** Monthly and at each change of personnel
- **Baseline:** Accountability elements recorded in the VOE at go-live
- **Alert rule:** Yellow: any required element older than its review interval m_Y. Orange: any element unassigned or unreachable. Red: no named owner or no decision authority while the system is in use.

- **Clinical importance:** Every escalation and intervention in the trigger ladder depends on a named person who can decide, so a lapse turns an alert into an unowned message.

- **Phase II scenarios:** primary responder in SC24; negative control in none

- **Governance action:** yellow: Review agent behavior and prompt; orange: Reduce autonomy level; require human approval for the action class; red: Halt the agent and escalate
- **Clinical importance:** Action authorization is the primary control on agentic clinical AI, and breaches indicate that the envelope is not holding.
- **Validation:** Follow-on generative or agentic study (outside Phase II) of simulated tool-permission drift in agent environments; detection of executed versus blocked breaches
- **Phase II scenarios:** none (outside the Phase II predictive anchor)
